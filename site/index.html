The Twenty-Year Letter

Letters to your child, in a file that outlives any company.

Write letters and keep them in one HTML file you own. It opens in any browser, with no account and no server. Save it to your own folder and back it up your way.

Download your file Try it live

How it works

  • Write a letter and save it straight to your file.
  • Your file carries the whole archive inside it.
  • Reopen it anytime, on any browser, years from now.

What you own, and what you keep

You keep the file, so you keep the backups. That is the point. Your letters live in your own folder, not on a service that has to survive two decades. The downloaded file starts empty and is yours to fill.

Open source. Yours to keep.
